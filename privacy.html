Privacy & Analytics

Respectful insight, used to improve the experience.

Essential analytics

With your permission, we use Microsoft Clarity to track essential analytics that help us understand how the site is used and improve its clarity, accessibility, performance and overall experience.

What analytics may include

  • Pages visited and general navigation through the site.
  • Interactions such as links selected, scrolling and feature usage.
  • General technical information such as browser, device type and approximate region.
  • Errors and performance information needed to keep the site reliable.

What we do not intend to collect through site analytics

We do not intend to use site analytics to collect names, email addresses, mobile numbers, dates of birth, authentication details, private messages or precise location.

Purpose

Analytics are used to understand what is helpful, identify where visitors encounter difficulty and guide improvements to Gita Jyoti. They are not used to sell personal information.

Your analytics choice

You can allow or decline analytics at any time. Declining analytics does not prevent you from using the site.
